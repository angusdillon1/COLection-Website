Voting

Does it need the internet?

Not in the room. Phones and booths connect to the host laptop over your local Wi-Fi. You need the internet for voting from home and for Google Forms imports.

Can people vote from home?

Yes. Sign in to your COLection account, open voting, then go to Voting > Online voting and press Start online voting. Each voter gets a personal link that works once. Booths keep working at the same time.

Can I run it all on the website instead?

Yes. COLection Online runs the same election in a browser. Move an election between the app and the website from the Account menu.

A booth froze, or a voter walked off.

The desk takes the ballot back and sends the voter again. Finished ballots stay safe on the booth until the host collects them.

Can we have more than one voting desk?

Yes. Other laptops join the host as extra desks with the pairing code. They share one voter list, so nobody is registered twice, and no desk sees how anyone voted.

Are phone voters ID-checked?

No. The ID check happens at booths, so use booths when you need it.

Setting up

Can I change the rules?

Yes, in the Rules step: positions, groups, quotas, ties and a minimum turnout. Every change is checked as you go.

We have thousands of members and no list.

On the Voters tab, tick Register voters at the desk as they arrive. The desk checks ID and registers people as they come, and only people who vote are kept.

Can I practise first?

Yes. Voting > Testing > Practice run opens a throwaway copy. Rehearse with booths and phones, then press Finish practice. Your real election is untouched, and practice is free.

Can I use Google Forms?

Yes. The Voting step imports a Google Forms file, merges different spellings of the same name and drops duplicate votes.

Can it email candidates and voters?

Yes. People > Emails > Set up sending walks you through Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or a work account.

Can my committee work on it with me?

Yes. Press Share and invite people by email. Make each one a Co-organiser, Editor, Runner or Viewer.

Licences and the rest

How do I add my licence?

Settings > Licence. Paste the line you were sent and press Use this licence.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Settings > Preferences > Appearance has four styles, light or dark, the text size and your own colours.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet. It's for Windows 10 and 11.

Before you start

  • It's built for in-person votes on a network you trust. Booth traffic is encrypted, but the phone page on your local network isn't HTTPS.
  • It counts the rules you give it. Check them against your constitution.
  • Keep the recovery key safe. Without it or the password, nobody can open the election.
  • The host laptop has to stay on, with COLection open, while people vote.
  • With desk registration, the voter limit counts people as they're registered. Size your licence for the turnout you expect.
