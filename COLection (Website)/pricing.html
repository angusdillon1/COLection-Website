Questions

Which plan do I need?

Count if people already voted, on paper or in a form like Google Forms. Vote if COLection runs the vote on the day. Complete if people also vote from home with a link. Every plan includes the official report and the results page.

What counts as a voter?

Anyone on an election's roll. A 500-voter licence covers any election with up to 500 people on the roll, as many times as you like.

We don't have a voter list.

Register people at the desk as they arrive. The limit only counts people registered, so you pay for who turns up. Over 5,000? Ask for a quote.

What if I pick a licence that's too small?

Nothing is lost, and the program tells you. Email us and what you paid goes towards the bigger one.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. Press the button above and your email opens with the details filled in. We send an invoice, then your licence once it's paid. Purchase orders are fine, or just email us.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Tell us within 14 days and we refund you in full.

Can I use it on another computer?

Yes. Paste the licence into any computer your organisation uses, including booths and a backup laptop.

Does it check the licence online?

No. It's checked on your computer. Nothing about your elections or voters leaves it.

What happens when it ends?

COLection goes back to the free version. Your elections, results and exports stay open.
