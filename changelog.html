What's new.

What changed in each version.

0.9

November 2026

Voting day

  • Connect booths, QR screens and extra desks before voting opens, and test each one from the desk.
  • Booths stay connected when voting closes, so reopening needs no re-joining.
  • Use a tablet or phone as the front-desk QR screen, with nothing to install.
  • Any free booth can show a voter's QR code. Each booth can show ballots, QR codes or both.
  • Optional time between voters: a short wait before the next ballot appears, and a rest after each voter.
  • Booths lock to the voting screen, and the desk is told if one is left.
  • A countdown across the top before voting opens and before it closes. Opening early asks first.
  • Voters missing a detail, such as International student, can still vote. The desk fills it in on the day, and online voters' details are checked before their ballot counts.

The ballot

  • Phones and online voting follow your ballot's look.
  • A smoother ballot on booths and phones: numbered badges, a progress bar and gentle movement, switched off for anyone who asks for less motion.

Nominations

  • Accepted applicants go straight onto the candidate list.
  • Ask a question only if an earlier answer fits, and let applicants finish later on any device.
  • Limit how many positions someone can stand for, and mark positions that can't be combined.
  • The form only offers positions the applicant can stand for.
  • A different profile for each position, if they like.
  • A deadline for confirming. New nominations come in by themselves while you're signed in.
  • Write your own wording for the outcome email.

People and lists

  • Big voter lists load fast: 5,000 names in a fraction of a second.
  • One column for each detail, with gaps highlighted, and Fill empty details to fill them in one go.
  • Two people with the same name but different IDs are kept as two voters.
  • Imported votes: a clear path through the five import parts, and people who filled in a second form are counted once for turnout.

Rules

  • "Nothing else": whoever wins this position can't hold any other.
  • One colour for the whole election, taken from your icon if you like. The ballot, the candidate form and the results page all follow it.

Count and results

  • See how long the count took. Everyone elected to a position sits together in one cell.
  • The quota and each round are explained in plain words, on screen and in the official report.
  • Turnout by group in the official report.
  • Landscape pages and smaller headings for wide tables in the data reports PDF.
  • A new results page, and a Showcase style that reveals the winners one position at a time. You can reveal each one live with a click.
  • Deleting an election asks for its exact name and its password.

Working together

  • Share an election with other COLection Online accounts and give each person a role: Co-organiser, Editor, Runner or Viewer. Changes reach everyone within seconds.

Smaller things

  • Marks in the step list show what's done, what's left and what needs a look. Click one to set your own.
  • The email window opens inside the election's window, with a preview of the email as it arrives.
  • Tick boxes under another tick box grey out with it. Closing a window with unsaved changes asks first.
  • A clear button in every search box, clearer dividers, a tidier time picker, toasts that stay longer, and a new splash screen.
  • Many fixes, including typing coming out backwards in the nomination form editor.

0.5

November 2026

A cleaner look, and six steps

  • A new, calmer look: white pages, a plain step list with a coloured icon for each step, and flat buttons. Three other styles (Classic, Studio, Glass) are in Settings > Preferences > Appearance.
  • The steps are now Rules, People, Voting, Check, Count and Export. Each step's smaller parts sit in the list down the left, so there are no rows of tabs.
  • Smoother, faster switching between steps, and sharper icons and corners on high-resolution screens.

The ballot

  • An optional welcome page before the first question, a description for each position, and a short line under each candidate's name.
  • Candidate profiles have their own page: each position has a "Vote" tab and an "About the candidates" tab.
  • Show the names in an order you set, for example to match your posters.
  • Change the instructions and the thank-you messages.
  • Approval voting: tick everyone you approve of.

People and emails

  • Email each candidate to confirm their nomination, from your own email account. Setting it up takes a couple of minutes, with the steps for Gmail, Outlook and others shown for you.
  • Remind people who haven't voted yet with one email from the voting desk.
  • The voting desk shows who has and hasn't voted at a glance.

Also new

  • Choose your ways of voting with tick boxes: stations, phones, online links, paper ballots and form responses, together or alone.
  • A clearer Check step, with a link from each finding to where it is fixed.
  • A richer profile editor with lists, tables and photo cropping.

0.2.3

October 2026

Vote from anywhere

  • Online voting needs a COLection account, and the COLection website that runs it is still being set up. Everything else in this version works without one.
  • Online voting: every voter gets a personal link, sent by email from COLection or copied and sent by hand. People vote from wherever they are, and a link works once, for one person.
  • It runs beside voting at stations. A voter who votes in person can no longer use their link, and a voter who has voted online cannot be sent to a station, so nobody votes twice. Online ballots come over with no names when you stop online voting.
  • COLection Online, in a web browser, now has the same screens as the desktop program, including a voting desk that shows a QR code for each voter to scan, and the same Preferences with sound effects. Your look and licence follow you between the two.

Changes

  • Signing in asks for the website address if this copy does not know it yet, and remembers it.

0.2.2

October 2026

Extra voting desks

  • Other computers on the same network can join the host as extra voting desks, so several people can register and send voters at the same time. The host still keeps the one list and the ballots.
  • A desk can search the list, register walk-ins, send people to a station or a phone, cancel a ballot and answer a call for help. It never sees how anyone voted.
  • Practice run: rehearse the whole election on a separate throwaway copy, with stations, phones and extra desks, made-up voters and random votes and a practice result. Your real election is never touched.
  • A new Joined devices tab lists every desk, voting station, QR screen and phone connected, and can disconnect all voting stations, all extra host computers or all QR screens at once.
  • Registering a walk-in whose name is already on the list now asks first, so nobody is added twice.

Changes

  • Seating plans are switched off for now, while COLection focuses on elections. Plan files you already made are untouched.

Fixes

  • An extra desk's voter list now shows straight away instead of staying blank until you type.
  • A computer set up to show only QR codes now shows a code that phones can open, even when it joined the host by an address that only the host could use.

0.2.1

October 2026

Big groups and licences

  • Walk-in registration for elections with no voter list: a desk registers people as they arrive and sends them to a station. The station can ask them to retype their student number or ID, and only a scrambled version of it is kept.
  • An installer for Windows, so COLection installs like any other program.
  • Licences can have a start date, can be copied from the program, and can be removed again (entering the same line brings it back).
  • Registering a walk-in voter respects the free version's voter limit.

0.2.0

October 2026

Seating plans

  • A new module for events: draw a floor plan with round, rectangular and banquet tables, walls, doorways and other elements.
  • Add guests by hand or from a spreadsheet, with groups, meal choices, dietary needs and RSVPs.
  • Say who should sit together or apart, and let Auto-arrange find the best seating. Lines on the plan show wishes met and missed.
  • Suggest swaps, snapshots with compare, and late changes: cancellations and plus-ones.
  • Find a guest from anywhere in the plan.
  • Print the floor plan, table lists, place cards and lookup sheets, with previews, your own logo and your choice of paper.
  • Snapping, rulers, distance labels and an alert when two tables overlap.
  • The free version allows up to 40 guests per plan for arranging and printing.

A new look

  • The whole program has moved to a new interface, with light, dark and match-this-computer appearance.
  • Opening New election, Voting station or Take over hosting now slides the form into the main window instead of opening a popup.
  • Each election can have its own icon on the home screen.

Step 5: Export results

  • A new step with every report setting in one place, and a live PDF preview that updates as you tick things.
  • Zoom the preview in and out, and make the text and pictures in the report bigger or smaller.
  • Un-ticking a heading, such as a position, greys out and un-ticks the choices under it.

Smaller fixes

  • The turnout bar no longer overlaps its heading on the Collect votes page.
  • The step icons are all the same size.
  • Switching between tabs while running a vote now slides smoothly without a faded strip at the edge.
