For Windows 10 and 11 · Free for up to 50 voters · No account needed

The whole election, in one program.

Nominations

Publish a form and candidates put themselves forward. You accept each one with a click, and the ballot builds itself.

Voting

Voters rank the names on their own phone — in the room or from home. Nothing to install, and it works even without wifi.

Counting

One click, counted — preferential votes, whole committees, ties. You get the winners on screen and a report you can print and sign.

Election night, without the pile of paper.

One laptop in the room runs the show. Voters scan a code and vote on their phones, or at a laptop set up as a booth.

  • Someone can't make it? Send them a personal link to vote from home.
  • Ballots carry no names and no times, so every vote is secret.
  • If a computer dies mid-election, no vote is lost.

Results no one can argue with.

COLection shows how every vote moved, round by round, so a close result is never a fight.
